# Data in this directory

These JSON files are generated by `pipeline/` (see the repository README) and
are derived from the following sources. Their licenses apply to the files here.

## Frequency counts — `chars.json`, `words.json`, `manifest.json`

Derived from: Tilman Schalmey (2021), *Raw frequency data: Thoughts on
"Reliable" Learner's Vocabularies for Classical and Literary Chinese*. Zenodo.
<https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5638882>

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

Changes: the two texts that occur in two corpora (史記, 漢書; identical copies)
are stored once; text titles are normalized (originals are kept as
`sourceTitle`; see `pipeline/texts.toml`); the per-text counts of characters
and of 2–4-character words are re-encoded as sparse matrices; derived columns of
the original CSVs (relative/cumulative/normalized frequencies) are dropped,
since the site recomputes them for any selection of texts.

## Character properties — `unihan.json`, and `radicals` / `blocks` in `manifest.json`

Derived from the Unicode Character Database 18.0.0 (Unihan, CJKRadicals.txt,
Blocks.txt). © 1991–2026 Unicode, Inc. Distributed under the Unicode License
v3, <https://www.unicode.org/license.txt>. A subset of fields is extracted for
the characters attested in the corpora.
